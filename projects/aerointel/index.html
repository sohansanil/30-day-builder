Why AeroIntel Exists

I built AeroIntel to explore how environmental data can be transformed into decisions people actually make every day. Instead of just showing raw chemical indices, AeroIntel combines statistics, regression forecasting, and database engineering to answer a simpler question:

"What does today's air quality mean for me?"
